## Project Name

Khadmat AI

## Short Description

Khadmat AI is a mobile-first agentic AI service orchestrator for Pakistan's informal economy. Users can request plumbers, electricians, AC technicians, tutors, beauticians, and other home services in Urdu, Roman Urdu, or English. The system extracts intent, discovers nearby providers, ranks the best match, simulates booking confirmation, and schedules follow-up reminders with full agent trace logs.
